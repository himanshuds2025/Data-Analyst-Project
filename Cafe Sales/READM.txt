Page 1 — Business Performance Overview

What is happening overall?

The business shows stable, predictable performance over time. Monthly revenue closely tracks transaction volume, indicating that growth is primarily volume-driven rather than price-driven. There are no extreme spikes or collapses, suggesting demand consistency and operational stability rather than short-term promotional noise.

So what?
The shop is not struggling with volatility. Optimization efforts should focus on incremental improvements (volume, mix, efficiency), not firefighting instability.

Page 2 — Product Intelligence (Signal vs Noise)

Which items actually matter?

Revenue is highly concentrated. A small subset of items consistently generates the majority of revenue, while the remaining products contribute marginally.

Top items are not one-off performers; their revenue remains consistent month over month, confirming they are real drivers, not seasonal flukes.

The revenue distribution shows a long tail of low-impact items that collectively add little value.

The price vs revenue relationship reveals a clear cluster of low-revenue items at lower prices, reinforcing their classification as operational noise.

So what?
Not all products deserve equal attention.
Inventory planning, pricing strategy, and promotions should prioritize high-impact items, while low-contributing items should be reviewed, bundled, simplified, or deprioritized to reduce operational complexity.

Page 3 — Customer Behavior & Channel Mix

How customers buy and pay

Customer behavior is structurally stable:

In-store transactions dominate consistently, with delivery acting as a steady secondary channel, not a volatile experiment.

Digital payments and cards account for most transactions, while cash remains a meaningful but declining minority.

No payment method or channel shows erratic swings, indicating mature, predictable customer preferences.

So what?
There is no urgency to overhaul channels or payment systems. The opportunity lies in incremental optimization—improving in-store efficiency, maintaining digital payment reliability, and selectively supporting delivery where it complements core demand.
