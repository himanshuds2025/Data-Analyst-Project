This was my most enjoyable project so far, personally I wanted the final output to have a vintage look to it, 
and I hope the insights are not being overpowered by the theme itself.


Cleaned and analysed in MySQL, performed EDA in Python notebook, finished with visualisation in PowerBI.



Tools Used:  DBeaver (for bulk import), MySQL, Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, PowerBI, Canva


Business Requirements

KPIs :
Total Books
Total Ratings
Average Rating
Rated Books (books with ≥1 rating)

Charts : 
Rating distribution (count of ratings by rating value / bins)
Top 10 books by rating count (popularity)
Top 10 books by average rating (with minimum rating count filter)
Books count by publication year
Books count by publisher (Top 10)
Books count by author (Top 10)

Dataset : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/saurabhbagchi/books-dataset
