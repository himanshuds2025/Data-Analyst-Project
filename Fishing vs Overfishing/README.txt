
this project was done to show the difference between sustainable fishing and overfishing around the globe both collectively and on country level.

Desired insights in the final dashboard:

1. global aspect
overall, sustainable fishing or overfishing in the world?
which oceans are at risk overfishing?

2. country level
which countries are fishing sustainably and which are overfishing
avg consumption of seafood /person/year by country

3.
types of fishes (visually explain the jargon to understand in a snap) that are captured



DATASET: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sergegeukjian/fish-and-overfishing?select=seafood-and-fish-production-thousand-tonnes.csv
